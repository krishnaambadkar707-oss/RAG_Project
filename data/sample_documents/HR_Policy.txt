# Global HR & Employee Policy Guide

Section 1: Annual Leave & Paid Time Off
1.1 All full-time employees receive 20 business days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
1.2 Unused annual leave up to 5 days can be carried over into the next calendar year, but must be taken by March 31st.
1.3 Leave requests longer than 3 consecutive days must be submitted via the HR Portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your direct manager.

Section 2: Remote Work & Hybrid Schedule
2.1 Employees are eligible for a hybrid work model allowing up to 2 days of remote work per week with manager consent.
2.2 Remote core hours are strictly between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST to ensure effective team communication.
2.3 The company provides a $500 one-time stipend for home office equipment setup upon joining.

Section 3: Parental & Caregiver Leave
3.1 Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following childbirth or adoption.
3.2 Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
3.3 Employee health benefits remain active during the entire duration of caregiver leave.
